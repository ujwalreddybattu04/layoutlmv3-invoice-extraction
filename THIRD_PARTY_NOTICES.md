It depends on the exact base checkpoint above. The training procedure and measured validation results are provided in `train.py` and `assets/adaptation/training_report.json`.

AI coding assistance was used for implementation, review, and documentation. The included outputs and training/evaluation reports were produced by executing the code. The design notes describe the actual implementation so each decision can be reviewed and explained.
